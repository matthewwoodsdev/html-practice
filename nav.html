A Real Nav Bar

The bar above is a list. Every nav bar you've ever clicked is one, underneath.

A list stacks vertically by default and shows bullets. Two rules change that: list-style none removes the bullets, and display flex turns the stack into a row.

The links have no underline until you hover, which is what makes them look like buttons rather than text.
